Privacy Policy

Flora AI — Plant Doctor & Leaf Pathology

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Flora AI ("we", "our", or "the App") provides automated leaf pathology diagnostics, ambient lux light metering, and personalized plant care reminders. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed by the application.

2. Camera & Photo Processing

Flora AI requests camera and photo library permissions strictly to capture or select images of plant foliage for disease pathology and light exposure calculation. Image data is processed securely over encrypted SSL connections. We do not use your plant photographs to identify individuals, nor do we sell, distribute, or monetize user imagery.

3. Data Collection & Local Storage

We believe in privacy by design. Flora AI does not require user accounts, email registration, or social media logins to use the core diagnostic features. Your collection of plants, nicknames, care logs, and watering schedules are stored locally on your device via secure client-side storage (LocalStorage). This data remains under your direct control at all times.

4. Notifications & Care Reminders

Flora AI utilizes on-device local notifications (via Apple's UserNotifications framework) to alert you when your plants need watering, misting, or recovery follow-up scans. No personal identifiers or notification logs are transmitted to external push marketing servers.

5. In-App Subscriptions & Payments

Subscription purchases, renewals, and restore transactions for Flora Pro are handled directly by Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, or financial billing information. Apple securely manages all transactions under the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your plant collection and care logs are stored locally on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over your data. You can delete any individual plant or reset your entire garden at any time within the application. Uninstalling the application completely erases all locally saved records. For any data inquiries, you may contact us at maks200r1@gmail.com.

7. Zero Tracking & No Third-Party Ads

Flora AI does not display third-party advertisements and does not participate in cross-app or cross-website data broker tracking. The App complies fully with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Flora AI does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or legal inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or Flora AI's privacy practices, please contact us directly at maks200r1@gmail.com.
