Privacy Policy

Flora AI — Plant Doctor & Leaf Pathology

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

Flora AI ("we", "our", or "the App") provides leaf pathology diagnostics, ambient lux light metering, and personalized watering intelligence. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed by the application.

2. Camera & Photo Usage

Flora AI requests camera and photo library permissions strictly to capture or select images of plant leaves for disease detection and lux calculation. Leaf images are processed securely using encrypted SSL connections and are not linked to your personal identity or sold to third parties.

3. Data Collection & Processing

We do not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identifying information to use the plant diagnostic features. Your collection of plants, nicknames, and watering logs are stored locally on your device via secure client-side storage (LocalStorage / iOS Keychain).

4. In-App Subscriptions & Analytics

Payment transactions, renewals, and restore purchases are processed securely through Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We do not process, store, or have access to your credit card or billing details. Apple manages all financial transactions in accordance with Apple Media Services Terms.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us directly via the app settings or at flora.app.contact@gmail.com.
